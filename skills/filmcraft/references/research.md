These are craft decisions derived from source material. An editorial technique that works in a particular film is a hypothesis to test in the next one. Research the subject of each new commission separately.

## Research a new subject

Start with one observable question. Locate the closest original record. Open it and record the paragraph, figure, demonstration step or tape time that supports the line. Check period, units and population. When accounts conflict, document the exact disagreement and test for a difference in dates, definitions or scope. Escalate to a second independent source when the outcome depends on a disputed claim. State what could change the conclusion.
